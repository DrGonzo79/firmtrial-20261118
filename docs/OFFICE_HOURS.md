# Office Hours diagnosis

## Customer and status quo

Start with a **CPA-credentialed operator who runs an accounting media/audit practice**, not a generic SaaS founder. The first paying customer is an AI-native accounting firm that needs credible distribution and assurance preparation. Today, firms buy leads from noisy directories while buyers infer quality from badges, referrals, and responsiveness; none exposes comparable work output.

## Desperate use case

An AI-native firm preparing for enterprise diligence or AICPA/AIUC assurance needs to find gaps in accuracy, review evidence, and data handling before the buyer or assessor does. “Get listed” is not desperate. “Avoid losing an enterprise deal or failing readiness review” is.

## Narrowest paid wedge

Sell a private, $750 same-case readiness assessment to 10 modern firms. Publish only with explicit consent. The public artifact is a methodology-led media scorecard; the paid deliverable is evidence gaps and retest readiness. Do not launch a broad marketplace.

## Observation plan

Run 15 interviews with AI-native firm founders and 5 buyers/procurement leaders. Ask for the last diligence questionnaire, lost-deal reason, or assurance-prep workflow. Then offer five paid pilots before building ingestion or matching. Track consent-to-publish, assessment completion time, paid conversion, and whether findings change a real control.

## Future-fit

If firms pay and buyers use the methodology, expand into retesting, assurance-prep workflow, and licensed benchmarks. If firms pay only for private readiness, kill the directory. If no one pays, keep the test as a media experiment rather than manufacturing SaaS demand.
